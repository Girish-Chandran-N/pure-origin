Environmental & Supply Chain Ethics

Upfront About Impact, Focused on Control

Trade across borders does have an impact, and we're upfront about that rather than glossing over it.

Sustainable by Default

What we focus on is the part within our control: choosing suppliers who treat people and materials properly, using eco-friendly packaging as standard rather than an extra, and grouping shipments to cut down unnecessary journeys.

We're a young company, and we're happy to say so — we'd rather be honest about being at the start of this work than pretend we've already arrived. It's part of what "Pure" means to us, and we intend to keep earning it.

Every claim we make should read like a promise the business is prepared to be held to.
