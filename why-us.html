7 Questions Every Buyer Should Ask

We know that choosing a new supplier comes with real questions. Here is how we answer the seven questions we hear most often.

1. Will what arrives match what we agreed?

Every batch is checked against your approved sample before it leaves our supplier's hands. If anything falls short, we hold it and tell you straight away — so what reaches you is exactly what you signed off on, every time.

2. Can we trust what we're told about where this came from?

Every product comes with clear origin information so you can speak to your own customers with real confidence about what you are selling.

3. Will someone actually answer, when it matters?

You'll have one accountable UK point of contact per order who knows your business and your shipment from start to finish — a real person to speak to, not a queue.

4. Will this clear customs without becoming a headache?

Export and customs documentation is prepared and checked before goods are sent, so the paperwork is sorted long before your shipment reaches port.

5. What happens if something runs late?

You hear it from us as soon as we know, not after — so you're never caught out by a problem we could have flagged earlier.

6. What will this actually cost, once everything's added up?

We cost things properly from the start — duties, logistics, all of it — so the number you're given is the number you can plan around.

7. If something goes wrong, who answers for it?

We do. Pure Origin is a UK company, and we stand behind everything we supply directly — no distant name to chase, just us.
